Device communications

Position
The nRF52840 bridge sends four SSI height readings to the viewer and carries the common target back to the motion controller over Bluetooth LE (Nordic UART Service) or wired serial.
Temperature and pressure
The ESP32 sends eight infrared temperatures and two pressure readings over a separate Bluetooth LE or wired serial link.
Thermocouples
Two USB TC-08 loggers each acquire eight K-type thermocouples.
Heating
The viewer sends four heater duty settings to the controller over serial. The displayed settings are requested duty cycles.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Four-axis motion control

Four screw drives move the diaphragm frames to a common height. A displacement sensor at each corner provides the feedback needed to keep the frames level.

Height tracking and differential levelling

Height tracking

The controller averages the four measured heights and compares that mean with the target from the viewer. The proportional gain Kp converts height error into a speed demand. The derivative gain Kd damps the approach using measured vertical velocity.

um=F(em)−Kd,zw¯,ui=F(em−δi)−Kd,zwi

Differential levelling

Each corner is compared with the mean height and mean velocity. A corner that runs ahead receives a slower command. A corner that lags receives a faster one. This correction reduces tilt while the frame moves.

ℓi=−λ(Kp,Lδi+Kd,L(wi−w¯))

In the simulated controller, each drive receives the average of the common and corner-specific tracking demands, plus its levelling correction. The controller limits the resulting speed and converts it to motor steps.

Tracking: vi*=um+ui2+ℓi,vi=Qsat(vi*)
z¯=z1+z2+z3+z44,em=zc−z¯,δi=zi−z¯

Here z is height and w is vertical velocity. An overbar denotes the four-corner mean. The reference height is zc. The proportional map F(e) clips Kp,ze to the speed limit, and λ scales the levelling correction with corner spread and distance to the target. Qsat limits and quantises the combined velocity.

During recovery, the reference height stays at the mean measured on entry. Stronger differential gains bring the corners together. Once the difference between the highest and lowest corners stays below 0.35 mm for 0.20 s, the reference ramps towards the requested height. During tracking, a spread above 0.80 mm triggers recovery again.

Control algorithm simulation and parameter optimisation

MOTION SIMULATION

For a 200 mm move starting with a 100 mm difference between corners, tuning reduced the final absolute mean-height error from 186.24 to 0.48 mm and the final corner spread from 0.185 to 0.035 mm. The tuned controller settled in 13.77 s.

The MuJoCo study tunes the balance between levelling and travel speed. Recovery prioritises corner alignment. As the frame approaches its target, the controller reduces the speed demand to limit overshoot. Twenty simulations tested a 200 mm upward move from initial corner spreads of 86.2–214.2 mm, with 100 ms sensor delay and 10 ms actuator delay.
